Multiplayer party brawler

Three choices.Eight players.Zero trust.

A multiplayer brawler with no attack button. Fight with Rock, Paper or Scissors.

Rock Paper Rumble turns the oldest game in the playground into a physical multiplayer arena. Bluff your hand. Read your friends. Get into position. When RUMBLE! hits, chase what you beat and run from what beats you.

No combos. No moveset. Three choices and eight people lying to each other.

Depth

Rock Paper Scissors isn’t the depth. The people are.

The rule is simple. Reading seven other people at once isn’t. Every flash is information. Every movement might be a bluff.

The flashShow them a hand. Make them react. Change it.
The late switchHide the real choice until the last possible second.
MemoryFour people switched. To what?
DistanceThe right hand in the wrong place is still the wrong hand.
The chainYou’re chasing someone. Someone’s chasing you. Someone else is chasing them.
The three-second allianceYou both want the same Rumbler gone. For now.

The rules never change. The situation never stops changing.

One rule everybody understands, applied by eight people at once, creates chaos that stays readable. When somebody gets launched, everyone knows exactly why, and usually whose fault it was.

Meet the Rumblers

Six Rumblers. Zero stat sheets.

Every Rumbler plays identically, deliberately. In a game about reading people, nobody should lose to a hidden stat. Pick the one you like, then make your friends regret theirs.

Bots

They know what you showed them. Not what you’re hiding.

Bots never peek at hidden hands. They play from the same information a human gets: the flashes they saw, the reveals they remember and where everybody is standing.

  • Remember stance flashes
  • Bluff and switch late
  • Chase winning matchups
  • Flee losing ones
  • Use power-ups
  • Avoid hazards
  • Play every mode’s objective

Development status

The game works. Now make it a product.

Rock Paper Rumble isn’t a concept searching for a game. The complete RUMBLE loop, seven modes, four arenas, six Rumblers, bots, couch multiplayer and online play are all in the build today.

v0.1 · advanced prototype · closed beta

Playable enough to put in front of people. Not a commercial release, and not pretending to be.

The core design is playable. The next phase is about turning that proof into a commercial product.

Art direction

The game is playable. Its final look is still open.

The prototype prioritised proving the game over producing final art. Some characters, UI illustration and environments currently use temporary or AI-assisted assets.

A commercial release would replace them with a cohesive, fully original visual identity created by professional artists. Today’s Rumblers and overall tone set the direction; they don’t lock the final look.

We proved the game first. Now the visual identity gets the same attention.

Why it can market

The audience already knows how to play.

Most multiplayer games have to teach the internet their rules. Rock Paper Rumble starts with something people already understand.

Hidden. Revealed. Resolved. Question marks create suspense. RUMBLE creates instant clarity. Then somebody goes flying.

The last-second switchThe three-way chaseThe alliance that lasted one revealThe Wild Card robberyThe Hot Potato handoffFloor panicLaunched into lava“YOU SAW ME SWITCH!”

The joke doesn’t need explaining. The viewer sees the mistake at the same moment the players do.
