What is

Sightline?

A logic puzzle about seeing straight. Numbers on the edge tell you how far a line of sight reaches into the grid. Find the bombs. Every level is solvable by pure deduction. No guessing, ever.

How it plays

3
Read the edge

Each number says how many tiles you can see along that row or column before something blocks the view.

Deduce, don't guess

Cross-reference the clues and every bomb reveals itself. There is always a next safe move, verified by a solver before a level ships.

Then it twists

Walls, channels, number tiles, mirrors, one-ways and portals bend the sightlines. Each arrives with its own teaching level.

In the hand

Everywhere you play

One game, one save

Progress follows the player automatically across their iPhones and iPads, and across their Steam machines. Linking a phone to a Steam Deck is a one-time code or a QR scan; after that it just syncs, and never backwards.

What's in the box

  • 400-level campaign, plus a Daily puzzle that is the same board for everyone on Earth that day
  • Time Challenge, achievements, leaderboards and friends via Game Center or Steam
  • On iPhone: the first 40 levels are free; Sightline+ unlocks the rest and cross-platform sync. On Steam it's the complete game.
  • Full controller support with a tile highlighter, board camera and Deck gyro; colour-blind palettes and reduced motion
