Something wrong? Write to me.

One person reads every message. No ticket system, no bot.

Report a bug

Crashes, a level that won't load, a button that does nothing — send it over. Three things make it far faster to fix, and they're all in one place: Settings → General → About on your phone, and the version number at the bottom of the game's own settings screen.

  • What happened, and what you were doing right before it.
  • Your device and iOS version.
  • The app version.

A screenshot or screen recording is worth a lot here, especially for anything visual.

Report a bug

A purchase didn't go through

First try Restore Purchases in the game's settings — that re-checks your receipt with Apple and is the fix in most cases. It has to be the same Apple ID you bought with.

If that doesn't work, email me and I'll sort it out. For a refund, Apple handles billing directly at reportaproblem.apple.com — I can't issue refunds myself, but tell me anyway so I can fix whatever caused it.

Get help with a purchase

I lost my progress

Progress is stored on your device, so it goes with the app — deleting and reinstalling clears it, and it doesn't follow you to a new phone. That's a real limitation rather than a bug, and it's on my list to improve.

If progress vanished without you deleting anything, that is a bug and I want to hear about it.

I'm stuck on a puzzle

Every puzzle in a Yumloop game is solvable by deduction alone — you never have to guess, and if a board seems to require guessing, that's a bug worth reporting. Send me the level number and I'll either walk you through the next step or fix the level.

Ask about a puzzle

Game Center and leaderboards

Leaderboards and achievements run on Apple's Game Center. If yours aren't showing, check you're signed in under Settings → Game Center. Signing in is optional — everything in the game is playable without it.

Feedback and ideas

Not everything is a problem. If something felt good, felt off, or you have an idea that would make a game better, that's the message I most want to get.

How long until I hear back

Yumloop is one person, so replies come when I'm at a desk rather than within a guaranteed window — usually a day or two. Every message gets read.
