Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

Last updated 21 November 2026 · Applies to Yumloop games and yumloop.net

The short version

Yumloop is one person. There is no sign-up form, no email address and no password to invent, and nothing here is sold or handed to advertisers. Your game progress lives on your device, backed up to your own iCloud, Google account or Steam Cloud. A small Yumloop account keyed to your Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam identity keeps the shared leaderboards, and, with Sightline+ (or on Steam), a copy of your save so it can follow you between an iPhone, an Android phone and a Steam Deck. You never type anything into it: the name other players see is the one you already use on Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam, and you can delete the account at any time from inside the game. If you play Sightline's online Elimination mode, that name is shown to the other players in your match, and the match itself is forgotten minutes after it ends. A small amount of anonymous usage data tells me which parts of a game people actually play, and that's the extent of it.

Your progress

Your progress, settings, and anything you've unlocked are stored locally on your phone, and your save is also backed up to your personal iCloud storage under your own Apple ID so it can follow you to a new device or survive a reinstall. That backup is handled entirely by Apple, lives in your iCloud, and is never uploaded to me — I have no way to read it. On Steam the same job is done by Steam Cloud under your Steam account, and on Android by Google's app backup under your Google account, handled by Google. Separately from those, Sightline can keep a copy of your save in a Yumloop account so that it can move between different kinds of device; that is described under Yumloop account below. Deleting the app removes the local copy; the iCloud backup stays with your Apple ID.

What leaves your device

Usage analytics

Yumloop games send anonymous gameplay events, things like a level being started or finished, or a menu being opened, through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. The events are aggregated and are not tied to your name, email, or advertising identifier. I use them to see where players get stuck and which features get used. No advertising or cross-app tracking identifier is collected, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown, because nothing here tracks you across other apps or websites.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you sign in to Game Center on iPhone and iPad, leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; on Android the same job is done by Google Play Games under Google's privacy policy. Your nickname on that service and your score are visible on leaderboards you take part in. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it.

Leaderboards

Everyone gets the same Daily Sightline, and the leaderboards are shared across iPhone, Android and Steam. When you finish a Daily or a level, your completion time, your star and level totals and your platform name are sent to the Yumloop account service described below so they can appear on the boards. With a level time the game also sends the moves you made (which tiles, in what order and when), so the service can check the time is genuine; the moves are used for that check and not stored. On iPhone your Daily time is also posted to Game Center's leaderboard under Apple's privacy policy. No email, real name or device identifier goes with any of it.

Multiplayer (Elimination)

Sightline's online mode runs each match on the same Yumloop account service. While you play, it receives your platform name, which kind of device you're on (iPhone or iPad, Android, or Steam), and your moves: the tiles you reveal, the boards you clear and the bomb that knocks you out, so it can keep every player's board in step and decide who wins. The other players in your match see your platform name, your device type, how far you've got and where you finished, and once you're out you can watch another player's board live, as they can watch yours. There is no text or voice chat. A private room is found by a short code you choose to share, and a room link opens a page on the same service that only shows that code. Everything about a match is deleted ten minutes after it ends, and a room invitation expires after ten minutes; no IP address is kept. Your results and win totals are kept with your account until you delete it.

Reporting and blocking

Tap another player in a multiplayer lobby or result to report their name or block them. Blocking is kept only on your device. A report sends the account service your account ID, the reported player's ID, the name as you saw it and the reason; I review reports by hand, and a name that breaks the rules is replaced everywhere by a generated stand-in such as Player 4821. The service also filters names automatically in the same way.

Friends from Game Center, Google Play Games and Steam

When you tap Invite friends in a multiplayer room, Sightline asks your platform for your friends list (on iPhone, only if you've allowed it to see your Game Center friends) and sends those friends' player IDs, the same pseudonymous IDs described under Yumloop account, to the account service. It checks which of them already have a Yumloop account. Because platform IDs are public, a friendship is only made when each of you has the other in your own platform friends list; until then your side is kept as a one-way note. IDs that don't match anyone are discarded, not stored. Nothing else about your friends is sent, and deleting your account removes these notes and links on both sides.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple or Google Play. I never see your card details, billing address, Apple ID or Google account. The store shares only whether a purchase was completed, which is what unlocks the content on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on streak reminders or update notifications, they're local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere, and they can be switched off in the game's settings or in your phone's notification settings at any time.

Fonts

Some screens load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see the request and the IP address it came from. No other information is sent, and I don't receive anything from it.

Feedback forms

If you follow an in-game feedback link, it opens a Google Form. Only what you choose to type into it reaches me, subject to Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you write to me, I have your email address and whatever you put in the message, for as long as I keep the thread. I use it to answer you and nothing else.

Yumloop account (cross-device sync)

Sightline uses a small account service I run on Cloudflare. It is keyed to a pseudonymous identity from the platform you're on — a Game Center player ID that Apple issues for this game alone, a Google Play Games player ID, or your 64-bit Steam ID, each proven to me by Apple, Google or Valve rather than typed by you. For every player it can hold your leaderboard times and totals, your name from that platform (filtered: a name that breaks the rules shows as a generated stand-in like Player 4821), your multiplayer results, and the friend code and friends list you build inside the game, including friends found through your platform as described above. With Sightline+ on iPhone or Android, or on Steam where it is included, it also holds your compressed save (levels, stars, streaks, statistics and settings — nothing that identifies you) and the time it was last updated, so progress can follow you between devices. No email, real name, IP address or device details are kept. Saves only ever move forward, so a linked device can never overwrite a newer save with an older one. Linking two devices uses a short code or QR that expires after a few minutes, works once, and has to be approved on the device that shows it; on iPhone the camera is used only while you're on the Scan a code screen, and nothing from it is stored or uploaded. Settings → Account also lets you unlink a device. When two accounts are linked, the save that isn't kept is archived for 30 days so a mistake can be undone, then deleted. To delete the account, see Deleting your account. The older save code transfer via dpaste.com has been retired and issues no new codes.

Test versions only

In test versions of Sightline (TestFlight, Google Play internal testing and the Steam beta branch), the game also records the moves of each level you play, so I can see where testers get stuck. They are sent with your account ID or an anonymous tester key, kept for 120 days, then deleted. A recording sent while signed out, from a device that has never signed in, isn't linked to anyone, so it can't be found to delete early; it goes at 120 days with the rest. Versions from the App Store, Google Play and the Steam store never record them.

Steam

On Steam, friends, multiplayer invitations, leaderboards, achievements and the cloud backup of your save are handled by Valve under Steam's privacy policy; the Steam version (Windows, Mac and Steam Deck) sends the same anonymous usage events as the iPhone version to TelemetryDeck, tagged with the platform they came from. The only identifier is a one-way hash of your Steam ID, so the same person on a Steam Deck and a PC counts once and the hash can never be turned back into an ID.

What Yumloop never does

  • No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the games.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No sign-up form, email address, or password anywhere in a Yumloop game, and nothing to type in: the name shown to other players is your platform name.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access. The camera is used only while you scan a link code, and never records or uploads anything.

This website

yumloop.net is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. GitHub keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, with the same consequence described above.

Children

Yumloop games are not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. There is nothing in a Yumloop game that asks a player for personal information at all, and online play has no chat: other players only ever see your platform name, filtered as described above. If you believe a child has sent me something through email or a feedback form, write to me and I'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything held on your device by deleting the app.
  • Delete your Yumloop account from Settings → Account → Delete account inside the game; the service forgets you at once. See Deleting your account.
  • Stay out of Game Center or Google Play Games by declining the sign-in prompt, or signing out in your phone's settings.
  • Ask about analytics. The usage events are anonymous and aggregated, so there is no per-person record for me to look up or delete. But if you have a question about what is collected, ask and I'll answer honestly.
  • Ask me to delete an email thread and I will.

If you're in the EU or UK, the lawful basis for the anonymous usage analytics is legitimate interest in understanding how the games are played; for email, it's answering the message you sent me.

Deleting your account

This covers Sightline on iPhone, iPad, Android and Steam, published by Yumloop.

In the game: open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. It takes effect at once, on every device linked to that account.

Deleted immediately: your save and any archived save, your leaderboard times and totals, your platform name, multiplayer results, friends, friend codes and friend notes, link codes and link requests, reports you made or that were made about you, test-version move recordings linked to your account or device, and the link between your Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam identity and the account.

Kept for 30 days: a one-way hash of your platform identity, so a deleted account can't be revived by mistake or misused straight away. It cannot be turned back into your ID and holds nothing else. After 30 days it is deleted too.

Not held by Yumloop: your iCloud, Google or Steam Cloud backup of your progress (delete the app or manage it in your device or Steam settings), Game Center and Google Play Games scores (managed by Apple and Google), and the anonymous usage analytics, which aren't tied to you.

Can't open the game? Email yoni@yumloop.net with the subject Delete my Sightline account and your Game Center, Google Play Games or Steam name. I'll delete the account and confirm by reply, within 30 days and usually within a couple of days.

Changes
