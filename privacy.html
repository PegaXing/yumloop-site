Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

Last updated 1 September 2026 · Applies to Yumloop games and yumloop.net

The short version

Yumloop is one person. There is no sign-up form, no email address and no password to invent, no profile to fill in, and nothing here is sold or handed to advertisers. Your game progress lives on your device, backed up to your own iCloud or Steam Cloud. If you use Sightline+ on iPhone or play on Steam, a small Yumloop account keyed to your Game Center or Steam identity can also keep a copy of your save so it can follow you between an iPhone and a Steam Deck; you can delete it at any time from inside the game. A small amount of anonymous usage data tells me which parts of a game people actually play, and that's the extent of it.

Your progress

Your progress, settings, and anything you've unlocked are stored locally on your phone, and your save is also backed up to your personal iCloud storage under your own Apple ID so it can follow you to a new device or survive a reinstall. That backup is handled entirely by Apple, lives in your iCloud, and is never uploaded to me — I have no way to read it. On Steam the same job is done by Steam Cloud under your Steam account. Separately from those, Sightline can keep a copy of your save in a Yumloop account so that it can move between different kinds of device; that is described under Yumloop account below. Deleting the app removes the local copy; the iCloud backup stays with your Apple ID.

What leaves your device

Usage analytics

Yumloop games send anonymous gameplay events, things like a level being started or finished, or a menu being opened, through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. The events are aggregated and are not tied to your name, email, or advertising identifier. I use them to see where players get stuck and which features get used. No advertising or cross-app tracking identifier is collected, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown, because nothing here tracks you across other apps or websites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible on leaderboards you take part in. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it.

Daily leaderboard

When you finish a Daily Challenge, your completion time and public Game Center nickname are stored in the game's shared iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so they can appear on that day's leaderboard, under Apple's privacy policy. No email, name, or device identifier goes with it, and skipping the daily means nothing is sent.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple shares only whether a purchase was completed, which is what unlocks the content on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on streak reminders or update notifications, they're local notifications scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere, and they can be switched off in the game's settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Fonts

Some screens load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see the request and the IP address it came from. No other information is sent, and I don't receive anything from it.

Feedback forms

If you follow an in-game feedback link, it opens a Google Form. Only what you choose to type into it reaches me, subject to Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you write to me, I have your email address and whatever you put in the message, for as long as I keep the thread. I use it to answer you and nothing else.

Yumloop account (cross-device sync)

Sightline+ on iPhone, and the Steam version, can sync your save through a small account service I run on Cloudflare. It holds only a pseudonymous identity from the platform you're on (a Game Center player ID that Apple issues for this game alone, or your 64-bit Steam ID, each proven to me by Apple or Valve rather than typed by you), your compressed save (levels, stars, streaks, statistics and settings — nothing that identifies you), and the time it was last updated. No email, name, IP address or device details are kept. Saves only ever move forward, so a linked device can never overwrite a newer save with an older one. Linking two devices uses a short code or QR that expires after a few minutes and works once; on iPhone the camera is used only while you're on the Scan a code screen, and nothing from it is stored or uploaded. Settings → Account → Delete account removes everything the service holds about you immediately; the older save code transfer via dpaste.com has been retired and issues no new codes.

Steam

On Steam, friends, leaderboards, achievements and the cloud backup of your save are handled by Valve under Steam's privacy policy; the Steam version sends no analytics at all.

What Yumloop never does

  • No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the games.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No sign-up form, email address, or password anywhere in a Yumloop game.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access. The camera is used only while you scan a link code, and never records or uploads anything.

This website

yumloop.net is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. GitHub keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, with the same consequence described above.

Children

Yumloop games are not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. There is nothing in a Yumloop game that asks a player for personal information at all. If you believe a child has sent me something through email or a feedback form, write to me and I'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything held on your device by deleting the app.
  • Delete your Yumloop account from Settings → Account inside the game; the service forgets you at once.
  • Stay out of Game Center by declining the sign-in prompt, or signing out in iOS Settings.
  • Ask about analytics. The usage events are anonymous and aggregated, so there is no per-person record for me to look up or delete. But if you have a question about what is collected, ask and I'll answer honestly.
  • Ask me to delete an email thread and I will.

If you're in the EU or UK, the lawful basis for the anonymous usage analytics is legitimate interest in understanding how the games are played; for email, it's answering the message you sent me.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it. Yumloop is a small operation and the honest summary is unlikely to move far from where it is now.
