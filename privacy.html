Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

Last updated 30 August 2026 · Applies to Yumloop games and yumloop.net

The short version

Yumloop is one person. There is no account to create, no profile to fill in, and nothing here is sold or handed to advertisers. Your game progress lives on your device. A small amount of anonymous usage data tells me which parts of a game people actually play, and that's the extent of it.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, and anything you've unlocked are stored locally on your phone using standard on-device storage. None of it is uploaded to me, and I have no way to read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Usage analytics

Yumloop games send anonymous gameplay events, things like a level being started or finished, or a menu being opened, through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. The events are aggregated and are not tied to your name, email, or advertising identifier. I use them to see where players get stuck and which features get used. No advertising or cross-app tracking identifier is collected, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown, because nothing here tracks you across other apps or websites.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center nickname and score are visible on leaderboards you take part in. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it.

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple. I never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple shares only whether a purchase was completed, which is what unlocks the content on your device.

Fonts

Some screens load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see the request and the IP address it came from. No other information is sent, and I don't receive anything from it.

Feedback forms

If you follow an in-game feedback link, it opens a Google Form. Only what you choose to type into it reaches me, subject to Google's privacy policy.

Email

If you write to me, I have your email address and whatever you put in the message, for as long as I keep the thread. I use it to answer you and nothing else.

What Yumloop never does

  • No advertising, and no ad networks embedded in the games.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No account, sign-up, or password anywhere in a Yumloop game.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.

This website

yumloop.net is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. GitHub keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, with the same consequence described above.

Children

Yumloop games are not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. There is nothing in a Yumloop game that asks a player for personal information at all. If you believe a child has sent me something through email or a feedback form, write to me and I'll delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything held on your device by deleting the app.
  • Stay out of Game Center by declining the sign-in prompt, or signing out in iOS Settings.
  • Ask about analytics. The usage events are anonymous and aggregated, so there is no per-person record for me to look up or delete. But if you have a question about what is collected, ask and I'll answer honestly.
  • Ask me to delete an email thread and I will.

If you're in the EU or UK, the lawful basis for the anonymous usage analytics is legitimate interest in understanding how the games are played; for email, it's answering the message you sent me.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it. Yumloop is a small operation and the honest summary is unlikely to move far from where it is now.
